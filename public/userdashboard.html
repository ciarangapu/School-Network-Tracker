User Guide

Access the user manual for network usage.

1. How can I check my current data usage?

To monitor your data consumption, you can log in to your account on the school portal or use a data tracking app on your device.

2. What should I do if my connection is slow?

If you're experiencing slow internet, try restarting your router, moving closer to the Wi-Fi source, or checking for background applications that may be using bandwidth.

3. How can I adjust my Wi-Fi settings?

Access your router's settings through a web browser using its IP address. Consult your router's manual for specific instructions on adjusting settings.

For detailed guidance, click here.

4. What to do if I can't connect to the internet?

Check your device's Wi-Fi settings, ensure the router is powered on, and look for any outages in your area. If issues persist, consult the troubleshooting guide.

For troubleshooting tips, click here.

5. How can I improve my network performance?

Consider using a wired connection for stability, limiting the number of devices connected, and ensuring your router firmware is up to date.

For more tips, click here.
